Sources, method and corrections

By Steve Hunt ACII TEP · Last updated 10 November 2026

Every page on this site is by Steve Hunt ACII TEP, who has worked in UK financial services since 1980. This page explains where the answers come from, how they are backed up, and what has been corrected.

How each answer is backed up

Beside each answer is a Sources line, and most pages end with a full list of their sources. Every source carries a label, so you can see what kind of authority stands behind each statement:

  • Law: Acts of Parliament and regulations, linked to the official text on legislation.gov.uk.
  • HMRC: HMRC’s manuals and guidance on GOV.UK: HMRC’s published view of how it applies the tax law. It is guidance, not law.
  • TPR, FCA, PRA, FSCS, ICO, DWP, HM Treasury, Bank of England, Ombudsman: what the regulators, government departments and other official bodies publish.
  • GOV.UK: other government guidance, such as rate tables.
  • MoneyHelper: the free guidance service backed by the government.
  • Provider evidence: fee sheets, published terms and written confirmations from providers, with the date they were checked.
  • Administrators: reports from the administrators of a firm that has gone into administration.
  • Published source: figures credited to the publication that reported them.
  • Steve’s analysis: Steve’s own arithmetic or reasoning, such as a worked example.
  • Steve’s view: Steve’s own opinion, in his own words.
  • This site: another page on this site that gives the detail.

Where the law is clear, it comes first, linked to the official text.

Dates

Each page shows the date it was last checked. That date changes only when the page has actually been reviewed. Tax rules change, so always check the date.

Steve’s 2022 blog posts

Steve’s blog posts from 2022 are kept substantially as he wrote them, with typing errors corrected. Where the old wording could now mislead, a dated 2026 note sits beside it. One 2022 post was withdrawn.
