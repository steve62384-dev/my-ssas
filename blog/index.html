My SSAS gives general information only. It is not advice or a personal recommendation. A SSAS is an occupational pension scheme, meaning a pension linked to an employer. It must be registered with HMRC to receive registered pension scheme tax treatment. If it has more than one member, it must also register with The Pensions Regulator.
