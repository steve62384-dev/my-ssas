Privacy policy

Last updated 10 November 2026

This policy explains what happens to your details when you visit this site or ask Steve a question. Your details go to Steve and Lisa Newmark, so Steve can answer you. Two of the email accounts that receive them are provided by Eadon & Co Ltd, trading as Raven Wealth. We do not sell your details. We will not pass them to another firm for advice or other services unless you agree.

Who is responsible

Steve Hunt ACII TEP runs my-ssas.co.uk and decides how your details are used. In data protection law, that makes him the controller. In this policy, “we” means Steve.

Contact Steve at Stable Cottage, Water Lane, South Witham, Grantham, Lincolnshire NG33 5PH, by email at steve@my-ssas.co.uk, or on 01733 602 071.

What we collect

  • When you use the form or the SSAS Test: your name and email address, and anything else you choose to give us. That can be your phone number, the best time to call, your business name, your question and your SSAS Test answers.
  • When you email or phone Steve: what you tell him.
  • When you visit the site: visit counts, from Cloudflare Web Analytics, which uses no cookies. See the cookie policy.

Please do not send health information, or other sensitive personal information, through this site.

Who sees your details

  • Steve Hunt, and Lisa Newmark, his administrator.
  • Eadon & Co Ltd, trading as Raven Wealth. Two of the email accounts that receive enquiries, Steve’s and Lisa’s work accounts, are held on its email system.
  • Anthropic Ireland, Limited, if Steve uses its AI assistant, Claude, to help with your enquiry. Anthropic then acts as a separate controller, under its own privacy policy.
  • Another firm, but only if you agree. For example, a regulated financial adviser, a SSAS provider or a life cover firm. Steve will tell you which firm before he passes anything on.
  • The service providers that run the form, the email accounts and the visit counts, listed below.
  • Anyone the law requires us to share them with.

Where your details go, and the safeguards

These organisations keep or handle data outside the UK. Each one relies on a legal safeguard for that.

  • Formspree, Inc.: runs the form, and keeps a copy of each form sent. Where: USA (Amazon Web Services). Safeguard: Formspree says it relies on standard contractual clauses.
  • Google: Gmail, one of the email accounts that receives enquiries. Where: USA and other countries. Safeguard: the UK Extension to the EU-US Data Privacy Framework.
  • Microsoft: Microsoft 365, for the other email accounts that receive enquiries. Where: USA and other countries. Safeguard: the UK Extension to the EU-US Data Privacy Framework.
  • Cloudflare, Inc.: counts visits to the site, without cookies. Where: USA and other countries. Safeguard: the EU standard contractual clauses with the UK Addendum.
  • Anthropic Ireland, Limited: Claude, the AI assistant Steve may use to help him read, sort and draft replies. Where: USA and other countries. Safeguard: Anthropic says it relies on adequacy decisions or standard contractual clauses.

UK law treats transfers to US companies that have joined the UK Extension to the EU-US Data Privacy Framework as adequately protected. For UK data, contract safeguards can use the UK International Data Transfer Agreement, or the EU standard contractual clauses with the UK Addendum. You can ask Steve for a copy of the safeguards used for any transfer.

How long we keep your details

We delete the enquiry records from this site 2 years after our last contact with you. That includes the Formspree copy and the email copies. The only exception is where the law requires us to keep something longer, or we need it for a legal claim.

If you become a client of another firm, that firm keeps its own records under its own privacy rules. If you ask us to delete your details sooner, we will, where the law gives you that right.

Your rights

You can ask to see your details, to correct them or to delete them. You can also ask us to limit how we use them, or object to how we use them. Where the right applies, you can ask for a copy to take elsewhere. If you gave your consent, you can withdraw it at any time.

To use any of these rights, email steve@my-ssas.co.uk. We will normally reply within one month. If the law allows us longer, we will tell you. No decision about you is made by a computer alone.

Do you have to give us your details?

No. But Steve needs a way to contact you, to answer your question.

Complaints

You have the right to complain to Steve about how your personal data is handled. You can do this by email, by post or using the contact form. He will acknowledge your complaint within 30 days. He will then look into it, and tell you the outcome without undue delay.

You can also complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office (ICO): ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint, or call 0303 123 1113.
