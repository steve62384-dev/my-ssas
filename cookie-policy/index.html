Cookies and visit counts

Last updated 5 October 2026

This site sets no cookies. It counts visits using Cloudflare Web Analytics, which Cloudflare says uses no cookies and does not track you as a person. You can switch the counting off on this page.

Cookies

This site does not set any cookies. It has no advertising, and it does not use Google Analytics.

Visit counts

To see which pages help people, and to improve the site, we use Cloudflare Web Analytics. When a page loads, its script sends Cloudflare information such as:

  • the page you looked at
  • the site that sent you here
  • your browser and operating system, and the type of device
  • how quickly the page loaded
  • your country

Cloudflare says it does not use cookies, browser storage or fingerprinting to identify visitors. We see statistics for the whole site, such as how many people read a page, broken down by things like country and browser. We use them only to improve the site, and Cloudflare handles them only to help us do that.

Why there is no cookie banner

Since 5 February 2026, the law has let a website count visits without asking first, if the only purpose is statistics to improve the site. The website must explain this clearly, and give you a simple, free way to say no. The counts must not be used to track or profile individual visitors. This site uses Cloudflare Web Analytics on that basis.

Switch off visit counting

Visit counting can be switched off here. This needs JavaScript to be on in your browser.

Switching off saves one small note in your browser, so the counting stays off on this device. That note is used only to remember your choice, not for counting. Nothing else is stored. If you clear your browser data, or use another browser or device, you will need to switch it off again.

If your browser sends a Global Privacy Control signal, we treat that as switching the counting off.

Sending a form

When you send a form, your browser goes to Formspree’s website to confirm it was sent. Formspree’s own cookie and privacy policies apply there. The privacy policy explains what happens to your details.

Links to other sites

Links to other websites, such as GOV.UK and HMRC’s manuals, take you to sites with their own cookie policies.
