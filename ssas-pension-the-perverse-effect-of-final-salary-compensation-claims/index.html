This post has been withdrawn

This page used to hold “The perverse effect of final salary compensation claims on SSAS pension”, an opinion piece Steve wrote in November 2022. It included views about whether some people should transfer out of final salary pensions. This site does not give pension transfer advice, so the post has been withdrawn.

For the current rules, see Can I move a final salary pension into a SSAS? in the FAQ.

You can also read Steve’s other posts, or ask Steve a question about SSAS pensions.

My SSAS gives general information only. It is not advice or a personal recommendation. A SSAS is an occupational pension scheme, meaning a pension linked to an employer. It must be registered with HMRC to receive registered pension scheme tax treatment. If it has more than one member, it must also register with The Pensions Regulator. If you send an enquiry, it goes to Steve Hunt ACII TEP, who will contact you.
