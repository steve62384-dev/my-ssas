How much does a SSAS cost? Provider fees compared, 2026

By Steve Hunt ACII TEP · Last checked 6 October 2026

The short answer: we checked the current fees of ten providers. For a SSAS with two members, the yearly fee is £800 to £2,420, plus VAT. Most also charge initial costs. Of those we can price, the highest in our simple example is £2,100 plus VAT. With VAT, the yearly fee is £960 to £2,904, and the highest initial cost we can price is £2,520. The lowest yearly fee is £800, from SSAS Pensions Limited and Whitehall Group.

How we compared them

We took one simple example and priced it the same way for every provider.

  • A new SSAS with two members.
  • It holds a bank account and one investment portfolio. No property, no loans.
  • We show the yearly fee and the initial costs separately. Initial costs are the set-up fee, plus any fee to open the investment account or make the first investment.
  • The table starts with the lowest yearly fee, because that is the fee you keep paying. Where two yearly fees are the same, the providers are in alphabetical order.

Some providers charge the yearly fee in advance, at the start of the year. Others charge it in arrears, after the work is done. So ask when the first one is due.

Every figure comes from the provider's own fee schedule, price page or written confirmation, checked on 5 and 6 October 2026. Highstone's fees, and Mattioli Woods' property and loan fees, were checked again on 10 November 2026. Fees change, so always ask the provider for its latest fees before you sign.

What to look at besides the price

  • Is a professional trustee included? Westerby, SSASCo, Highstone and Howden include one in the fee. SSAS Pensions Limited acts as special trustee. Some others charge extra, or do not offer one.
  • Who is the scheme administrator? This is the legal role that deals with HMRC. Some providers take it on. Others share it with the trustees, or only help them with it. See who runs a SSAS.
  • How many members does the fee cover? Highstone and SSAS Practitioner.com charge the same for up to four members. Westerby and SSASCo charge for each member.
  • What do property and loans cost? Most providers charge extra. For example, Highstone charges £750 for a property purchase and £500 for a loan. Mattioli Woods charges £1,190 for a property purchase, then £740 a year for each property. For a loan to your own company, it charges £280 an hour to set the loan up, then £370 a year.
  • Must cash be left in the bank account? Whitehall asks for at least £1,000 (£5,000 if the scheme holds property or other hard-to-sell assets). Morhart asks for £1,500.
  • Do the fees rise every year? Some rise automatically, for example with wages or prices. Ask before you sign.
  • Can you go to them direct? Some providers only take schemes through a financial adviser.
